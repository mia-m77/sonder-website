ABOUT SONDER

Find your frequency.

SONDER is a student-built prototype for discovering independently released music. Its official Apple Music embeds provide previews, or full playback when available to eligible signed-in subscribers. Likes and listener interpretations are saved in this browser only; they are not shared with other users.

Song-story fields are optional. We do not invent quotations or stories from real artists.
